# Changelog

## F11 1.0 — 15 November 2026

First public release. **One life. Stop the scroll.**

### Features
- **Scroll awareness**: live swipe counter at the top of Reels and Shorts, even with the guard off.
- **Swipe budget**: a set number of swipes per session, then a full-screen shield for a cooldown.
- **Feeds only**: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight count; DMs, stories and regular videos stay open.
- **Block completely**: optionally block Reels and Shorts on the first swipe.
- **Max Focus**: block guarded apps entirely, including DMs, stories and videos.
- **Focus schedules**: Max Focus on a timetable (Work, Study, Sleep…); a running schedule can't be ended early.
- **Intentional friction**: loosening a rule takes a 30-second pause and typing a strong line; optionally say a second line out loud (on-device speech).
- **English and Hindi** strong lines.
- **Report**: 7 and 30 day history, daily average, busiest day, shields and focus streak.

### Privacy
- No internet permission. No ads, analytics or accounts.
- Reads only app names and view IDs, never on-screen text.

### Install
1. Download `F11-v1.0.apk` below and open it. Allow installing from your browser if Android asks.
2. Open F11, tap **Enable**, read the disclosure and turn on **F11 Shield** in Accessibility settings.
3. Android may show "Restricted setting" for sideloaded apps. If so, open **Settings → Apps → F11 → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings**, then try again.

Requires Android 8.0 or later.
